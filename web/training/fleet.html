What learned together

Every rollout advances all five learning policies in the same physical worlds. Each family has its own PPO optimizer and value function. Clear-route training progresses to opposing traffic and full missions. These policies learn individual navigation objectives; team tactics and combat are not part of this task.

Command the fleet

Click a vehicle in Map Lab, select a family, and right-click a destination. Air and submarine units support explicit height/depth targets. Sensor equipment changes what the policy observes; range overlays only change what you see. Fixed-wing routes include heading and physical turn/climb limits.
