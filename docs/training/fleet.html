128 scenarios per family across eight unseen maps. Contact counts are blocked simulation decisions, not unique impacts. A fixed-wing collision ends the episode. The reference steers toward waypoints without obstacle avoidance.

What learned together

Variants within a family share policy parameters. Each vehicle has its own observations, navigation goal and recurrent state. These runs train families separately against kinematic traffic; the other vehicles are not learning simultaneously.

Current limits

These are experimental local controllers. Single-seed training and short route sections do not establish reliable indefinite navigation. In a two-minute full-fleet probe, the hauler stalled and one transport collided. The browser provides an explicit Restart vehicles control.

The flight graph searches position and altitude; it does not guarantee a feasible turning radius. Waypoint localization and visible-neighbor velocity are ideal. Cosmetic trees and rocks are not collision bodies.
