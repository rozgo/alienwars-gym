LIVE IN THE BROWSER

One seed. A world of decisions.

Wave Function Collapse connects terrain, road grades and tunnel profiles. Irregular oval coastlines, graded beaches, lakes and hills shape the battlefield. Choose a coherent Temperate, Desert or Frozen palette, then explore symmetric and asymmetric layouts.

Grown bodies. Living materials.

All twelve fleet roles now have distinct biological bodies, with grown nursery forms at both bases. Temperate fungal parasols, Desert mushroom-cacti and Frozen frost harps replace traditional trees. Four 1K material scans, deeper shadows, moving shore foam and boat wakes add physical detail. Play view gives the battlefield more room.

Look inside a living world.

Open the official Flecs Explorer beside the battlefield. Inspect live body state, mission progress, sensor equipment and PPO observations from the same running simulation.

Take the controls.

  1. Move around: drag to pan, Shift-drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
  2. Command: click a vehicle, or select its family, then right-click a destination.
  3. Observe: follow a unit, toggle its sensors, or reveal the underground network.
  4. Regenerate: choose a palette and create a new symmetric or asymmetric world.
